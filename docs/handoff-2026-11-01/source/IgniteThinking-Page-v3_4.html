Why reasoning?

Your child already has tools that produce answers, and tools that will show the reasoning behind them too. IgniteThinking gives them practice at the part we choose not to outsource: framing a problem, trying an approach, noticing when it fails, revising it, and explaining why the one that works, works.

Why grades 4 and 5

Grades 3 to 5 are when children begin to justify why something is always true, instead of relying on a single example. NCTM 2000 That is the adaptive reasoning the National Research Council counts as a core strand of mathematical proficiency. NRC 2001 Grades 4 and 5 sit inside that window. Reasoning does not begin here. It is where we start.

A consistent thinking loop

Every puzzle runs through the same steps, so your child is never working out how the app behaves. The puzzles change. The thinking does not.

Understand

Read the situation. Say what is actually being asked before touching a method.

Define

Name the real goal. Most answers that miss are answers to a different question.

Ideate

Put down more than one way it could be done. One idea is not a choice.

Prototype

Build the attempt, with a picture or a tool to think with.

Test

Check it. An attempt that does not work is not the end. It is information, and the child reads it.

Always available

Reflect

Reflection closes every puzzle, and a child can reach it without having solved anything. A child who stays stuck is never handed the answer to move on.

See the thinking, not just the score.

You get your child’s thinking, in their own words, next to a plain view of how much help they used, so you can ask a better question at dinner.

  • The journal: what your child wrote at each step, kept in their own words.
  • Which puzzles were solved outright, and which were solved with help.
  • Whether your child is needing less help over time, and whether a later attempt was completed without opening any help.
  • The reflections, which are often the most interesting thing on the page.
  • Read-only, always. Nothing here lets a grown-up edit a child’s work.

What we will say plainly

The learner needs us less.

No chat box, and nothing generated on the spot: every word of guidance your child sees was written in advance by our team. We measure what matters most to us: whether your child needs less help as they go, and still solves without it.

Independent reasoning is what the app is designed to build, and we will report it once we have measured it.
